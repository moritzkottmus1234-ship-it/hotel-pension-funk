The House

Where Asta Nielsen held her salons

A Gründerzeit house from 1895, full of Art Nouveau detail. Its Beletage, the grandest floor, was home to the Danish silent film star Asta Nielsen until 1937. At the time she was one of the most famous women in the world.

Her salons drew artists such as the poet Joachim Ringelnatz and the actor Heinrich George. After the Nazi era, the apartment became the Hotel-Pension Funk, named after its first managers, the Funk sisters.

Much of the original interior survives. What is missing, we fill with Art Nouveau, Gründerzeit and Neo-Chippendale pieces, carefully restored. Photographers love it too: Vogue and Cosmopolitan have shot here, with Kate Moss and Claudia Schiffer in front of the camera.

  1. 1895The Gründerzeit house on Fasanenstraße is built.
  2. Until 1937Asta Nielsen lives on the Beletage. Her salons are a meeting place of intellectual Berlin.
  3. After the Nazi eraThe Funk sisters open the guesthouse in the apartment.
  4. Today14 rooms, a grand breakfast salon and a pianola from 1912. Family-run, with personal service.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Your question is not here? Call us: +49 30 8827193

Do all rooms have a private bathroom?

Not all. Many rooms have their own shower and WC. The less expensive rooms have a washbasin in the room, with shower and WC separately in the hallway. When you book, you see exactly which option you choose.

Why is there no TV?

On purpose. To us, quiet is the greatest luxury in a big city. There is free Wi-Fi all the same.

Can I reserve a specific room?

Unfortunately not. Every room is furnished differently and the photos show examples. We are happy to note your wishes, ideally by phone or through the request form.

When can I check in and out?

Check-in is from 8 am to 5 pm, check-out by 11 am. If you arrive after 5 pm, please let us know in advance, by phone or when booking.

Is breakfast included?

You can add the breakfast buffet if you like, currently €12 per person. It is served every morning in the historic breakfast salon.

Can I bring my pet?

Pets are welcome on request. Please ask us first, fees may apply.

How do I get here from BER Airport?

The S-Bahn takes about half an hour to Zoologischer Garten station. From there it is about a ten-minute walk. Uhlandstraße U-Bahn station is about 200 metres from the house.

Can I stay longer or rent a room as an office?

Yes. Our rooms are also available for long stays or as an office. Write to us and we will send you a quote.
